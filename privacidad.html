Privacy Policy

App: Control de Gastos (Android) · Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Control de Gastos Android app ("the app", "we") handles, how it is used, where it is stored, with whom it is shared, and how you can delete it. Control de Gastos is a personal-finance tool that helps you track installment purchases, fixed monthly expenses and the amount each person owes. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer, reachable at gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The data you enter stays on your phone. We run no servers and we do not receive your data.
  • If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app saves a backup file in your own Google Drive so you can restore or sync your data on your other devices.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, we show no ads, and we use no analytics or tracking tools.

3. Google user data and Google Drive access

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you tap "Connect with Google" in Settings → Sync, the app asks for this single permission (OAuth scope):

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app."

With this scope, the app can only access files that the app itself created. It cannot see, list, read or modify any of your other files or folders in Google Drive.

What the app does with it: it creates one file named control-gastos.json in your Drive containing the data described in section 2, and it reads and updates that file to keep your devices in sync and to let you restore your data. The app also reads the email address of the Google account you connected, to show it on screen.

Limited Use disclosure: Control de Gastos's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, the information received from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup and sync feature that is visible in the app; it is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide that feature or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads; and it is not read by humans, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

4. How we use the information

  • To calculate and display your monthly summary, installments, fixed expenses, the amount per person and your balance.
  • To save your data on your phone between sessions.
  • If you connect Google Drive: to back up your data and synchronize it between your devices, merging changes made on different devices.

We do not use your information for any other purpose, such as advertising, profiling, credit decisions or training machine-learning models.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose your information to third parties. The only network communication the app performs is with Google (Google Sign-In / authorization and the Google Drive API), and only when you have connected your Google account. That communication uses HTTPS. Your data in Google Drive is subject to Google's own privacy policy and security measures. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • On your phone: data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Protect your device with a screen lock.
  • In Google Drive: the sync file lives in your account and is protected by your Google account security.
  • Retention: your data is kept until you delete it. The developer does not keep any copy, since the data never reaches the developer.

7. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Edit or delete entries: you can edit or delete any purchase, expense, person, card or merchant inside the app.
  • Stop syncing: in the app, go to Settings → Sync → Disconnect.
  • Delete your Drive data: delete the file control-gastos.json from your Google Drive.
  • Revoke access: remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.

Because the developer does not hold your data, deleting it through the steps above removes it completely. For any request about your data you can also write to gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date. If the changes affect how Google user data is used, the app will not apply them to data you have already provided without asking for your consent again.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.
